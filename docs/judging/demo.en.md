## Capture and editing notes

Record the chosen revision and real channel evidence first. Keep the board,
choice numbers, vote changes and resulting sequence readable together. Use the
actual timing of the clip; a shortened montage must not imply an instant vote or
hide a different outcome. A two-choice round is valid; do not invent a third
card for the edit.

Capture genuine current Codex development/review work in the verified
[primary session](session.md). This overnight work did not produce a Codex video
recording, so retrospective narration alone does not establish that shot. The
owner can record further real review/fix work in this same thread. Avoid
exposing activation codes, authorization material, private configuration or
unrelated conversations.

Keep original source clips and a simple timecode/edit list. Review participant
recording scope, any visible third-party material and audio rights before
publishing. No soundtrack or generated voice is needed. The owner reviews the
final runtime and YouTube viewing settings, then adds the actual URL to the
submission checklist. There is no uploaded video or verified URL here.
